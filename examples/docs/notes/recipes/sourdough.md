# Sourdough bread

## Starter

Feed the starter with equal weights of flour and water twelve hours before baking. It is ready when it has
doubled and a spoonful floats in water.

## Dough

Mix 500 g bread flour, 350 g water, 100 g active starter and 10 g salt. Stretch and fold four times during
the first two hours, then let it rise until it has grown by half.

## Baking

Bake in a preheated Dutch oven at 250 °C for 20 minutes with the lid on and 20 minutes without.
